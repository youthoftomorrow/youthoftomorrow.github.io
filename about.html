About Youth of Tomorrow

A volunteer-run organization in Iowa City giving young people a crew, a mentor and a place to belong.

A crew for every kid, rooted in community.

Youth of Tomorrow started with a simple idea: young people do better when they have friends to show up with and adults who show up for them. We work alongside families and the Sudanese community in Iowa City to give boys and girls, from grade-schoolers to high-schoolers, safe and joyful things to do together.

Some nights that looks like a lane at the bowling alley. Other days it's a talk on the challenges young people face during Ramadan, a résumé workshop, or a tug-of-war in City Park with three generations cheering on.

  • Address 1556 S 1st Ave #C, Iowa City, IA 52240
  • Ages 12–18, plus family events for everyone
  • Open to boys and girls of every background

Mission, vision & goals

Structured, engaging activities for young people ages 12–18 that keep them on track academically, socially and personally.

Our mission

To support and empower young people to graduate from high school and pursue higher education, while fostering strong character, discipline and positive life choices.

Our vision

A safe and supportive community where every young person thrives academically, socially and emotionally, and is ready for college and a successful future.

Our goals

  • Graduate from high school
  • Go on to college and higher education
  • Build strong character and discipline
  • Make healthy, drug-free choices
  • Grow a strong foundation of faith and ethics
  • Stay safe and focused online

How we get there

  • Sports & physical activities

    Teamwork, discipline and healthy lifestyles through organized sports and fitness.

  • Character building & leadership

    Responsibility, integrity and perseverance, learned through mentorship and group activities.

  • Religious & ethical guidance

    Spiritual and moral teaching that gives young people a strong foundation of faith and ethical decision-making.

  • Lectures & workshops

    Sessions on staying drug-free, resisting negative peer pressure and making responsible choices.

  • Digital awareness & internet safety

    Using the internet responsibly, protecting privacy and limiting online distractions.
