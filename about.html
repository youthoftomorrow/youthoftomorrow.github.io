About Youth of Tomorrow

A volunteer-run organization in Iowa City giving young people a crew, a mentor and a place to belong.

A crew for every kid, rooted in community.

Youth of Tomorrow started with a simple idea: young people do better when they have friends to show up with and adults who show up for them. We work alongside families and the Sudanese community in Iowa City to give boys and girls, from grade-schoolers to high-schoolers, safe and joyful things to do together.

Some nights that looks like a lane at the bowling alley. Other days it's a talk on the challenges young people face during Ramadan, a résumé workshop, or a tug-of-war in City Park with three generations cheering on.

  • Address 1556 S 1st Ave #C, Iowa City, IA 52240
  • Ages 10–18, plus family events for everyone
  • Open to boys and girls of every background

What we believe

  • Belonging comes first

    Kids who feel they belong somewhere are more likely to stay in school, stay out of trouble and help others.

  • Faith builds character

    We help young people carry their values with confidence: at school, online and with friends.

  • Families are part of it

    Boys and girls of every background are welcome, and parents, grandparents and little ones join in whenever they can.

Executive Director

Nasr Aldden Osman Oshar Mohammed

Nasr came to the United States in 2012 through the Diversity Visa lottery and made Iowa City his home. He has worked in tech for 10 years and is a Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) at Leepfrog Technologies.

Long before Youth of Tomorrow, Nasr was serving his neighbors. From 2020 to 2023 he was a commissioner on Iowa City's Housing and Community Development Commission (HCDC), and he served the Sudanese community in Iowa City as its general secretary.
