Where your gift goes

Your gift pays for programs for young people in Iowa City, including:

  • Bowling, laser tag and trampoline park tickets
  • Rides to and from events
  • Food for iftars, potlucks and Eid celebrations
  • Chess sets, prizes and workshop supplies
  • Starting the new programs we're planning

Donate with Zelle

No fees. Zelle is built into most US banking apps.

Send to youthoftomorrowic@gmail.com
  1. Open your bank's app and choose Send with Zelle.
  2. Paste youthoftomorrowic@gmail.com as the recipient and check the name that appears before you send.
  3. Enter any amount and add “Donation” in the memo.
