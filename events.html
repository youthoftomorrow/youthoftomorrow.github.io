Swimming

We've partnered with the Iowa City Eels swim club to offer swim lessons for boys and girls ages 10–18. Every swimmer starts with a tryout, and the club then places them in the right group. Swimmers train with the club's coaches three days a week.

  • Certificates from the club
  • Modest, covered swimwear welcome for girls
  • Boys and girls can swim separately

You don't choose a group when you register. The Iowa City Eels place each swimmer in a group after a tryout.

Good to know

  • Liability insurance: $35 per swimmer, once a year. Required for every swimmer.
  • Equipment: the club provides it. Each swimmer only needs their own fins ($10–12).
  • SafeSport training: the club requires it for parents and for swimmers 12 and older.
  • Certificates: the club gives swimmers certificates.
  • Modest swimwear: girls are welcome to dress modestly and stay covered in the pool.
  • Boys and girls separately: the club is willing to separate boys and girls.
  • Tryout first: the club holds a tryout and assigns each swimmer to a group.
  • Who can join: boys and girls ages 10–18.

Games at the center

Foosball

Foosball showdowns

Our youth have had a blast at the foosball table in our center. Older teens team up with the younger kids, everyone takes a turn, and the cheering can be heard down the hall.

It's a fun way to make friends, practice teamwork and learn to win (and lose) with good sportsmanship.
