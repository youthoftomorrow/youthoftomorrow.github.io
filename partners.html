Our partners

Organizations we've worked with, and look forward to working with again and again, to keep our young people and their families healthy.

RIVA: Refugee & Immigrant Voices in Action

RIVA is a grassroots coalition led by refugees and immigrants in Iowa. It helps communities across the state reach opportunities and services, build workforce skills and grow their own leaders, through RIVA AmeriCorps, a statewide health equity campaign and an incubator for community-based organizations.

What we did together
A measles awareness campaign for immigrant and refugee families in our community, and a youth workshop on careers and interview skills.
Looking ahead
We look forward to more health campaigns with RIVA for the families we serve.
Visit rivaiowa.org

Iowa Dental Association

The Iowa Dental Association is the professional association for dentists across Iowa. Alongside supporting dentists, it helps Iowans find dental care and runs public programs that improve oral health in the state.

What we did together
A dental awareness program for kids, teaching healthy habits early.
Looking ahead
We look forward to bringing dental health days back to our kids and families again and again.
Visit iowadental.org
