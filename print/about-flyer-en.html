Growing up together in Iowa City.A volunteer-run nonprofit for youth ages 12–18 and their families.

A crew for every kid, rooted in community.

We work alongside families and the Sudanese community in Iowa City to give boys and girls safe, joyful things to do together, and adults who show up for them.

Our mission

To support and empower young people to graduate from high school and pursue higher education, while fostering strong character, discipline and positive life choices.

Our vision

A safe and supportive community where every young person thrives academically, socially and emotionally, and is ready for college and a successful future.

Our goals

  1. 1Graduate from high school
  2. 2Go on to college and higher education
  3. 3Build strong character and discipline
  4. 4Make healthy, drug-free choices
  5. 5Grow a strong foundation of faith and ethics
  6. 6Stay safe and focused online

How we get there

  • Sports & physical activitiesTeamwork, discipline and healthy habits
  • Character & leadershipResponsibility, integrity and perseverance
  • Religious & ethical guidanceFaith and ethical decision-making
  • Lectures & workshopsDrug-free living and resisting peer pressure
  • Digital awarenessInternet safety and protecting privacy
