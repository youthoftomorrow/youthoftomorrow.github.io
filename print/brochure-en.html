Who we are

A crew for every kid, rooted in community.

Youth of Tomorrow is a volunteer-run nonprofit in Iowa City. We work alongside families and the Sudanese community to give boys and girls safe, joyful things to do together, and adults who show up for them.

Our mission

To support and empower young people to graduate from high school and pursue higher education, while fostering strong character, discipline and positive life choices.

Our vision

A safe and supportive community where every young person thrives academically, socially and emotionally, and is ready for college and a successful future.

Youth ages 12–18Boys and girlsFamily events for all

What we work toward

Our goals

  1. 1Graduate from high school
  2. 2Go on to college and higher education
  3. 3Build strong character and discipline
  4. 4Make healthy, drug-free choices
  5. 5Grow a strong foundation of faith and ethics
  6. 6Stay safe and focused online

How we get there

  • Sports & physical activitiesTeamwork, discipline and healthy lifestyles.
  • Character building & leadershipResponsibility, integrity and perseverance through mentorship.
  • Religious & ethical guidanceA strong foundation of faith and ethical decision-making.
  • Lectures & workshopsStaying drug-free and resisting negative peer pressure.
  • Digital awareness & internet safetyUsing the internet responsibly and protecting privacy.
