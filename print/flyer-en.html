Swim with the Iowa City Eels

Swim lessons for boys and girls ages 10–18, three days a week, in partnership with the Iowa City Eels swim club.

Why families love it

  • Coached by the Iowa City Eels swim club
  • Swimmers earn certificates from the club
  • Girls are welcome to dress modestly and stay covered
  • The club is willing to separate boys and girls
  • The club provides the equipment

Every swimmer starts with a tryout. The club then places them in the right group, so you don't pick a group when you register.
